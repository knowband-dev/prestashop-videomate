# **PrestaShop Free Video Manager v1.0.0**

PrestaShop Free Video Manager by Knowband helps store owners display engaging video content on their eCommerce stores. It allows merchants to showcase product videos, reels and customer testimonials to make product pages more interactive and help customers understand products better.

Instead of relying only on product images and text descriptions, store owners can use videos to present products, highlight their features and share customer experiences directly on their storefront.

**Key features of the PrestaShop Video Manager Module include:**

**Product Videos**: Display product-related videos to give customers a better understanding of product features, usage and benefits.

**Video Reels**: Showcase short-form video content in an engaging format to make the storefront more interactive.

**Customer Testimonials**: Display customer testimonial videos to share real customer experiences and build trust among potential buyers.

**Multiple Storefront Placements**: Display videos across different store pages, including product pages, category pages and the homepage.

**Engaging Shopping Experience**: Use video content to make product discovery more interactive and provide customers with additional product information.

**3.0 Installation**

Go to Module Manager in your PrestaShop back office and click Upload a module to upload the module ZIP file. Once the installation is complete, click Configure to open the module.

Configure the video settings and add the video content you want to display on your storefront.

Select the relevant video placement options, such as product pages, category pages or the homepage, according to your requirements.

Save the settings and check the storefront to ensure that the videos are displayed correctly.
